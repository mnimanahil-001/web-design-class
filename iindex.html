Mary Shelley

Introduction

Mary Shelley was a famous English novelist born in 1797. She is best known for her novel Frankenstein, published in 1818. Her writing explores science, human nature, ambition, and the consequences of human actions. She is considered an important figure in Gothic literature and early science fiction.

Famous Literary Works

  • Frankenstein (1818)
  • The Last Man (1826)
  • Valperga (1823)
  • Lodore (1835)
